# Business Sales Performance Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data to understand sales trends, product performance, and regional profitability.

## Objective
To transform raw business sales data into useful insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- Superstore Dataset

## Analysis Performed
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Profit by Region
- Sales by Region
- Product-wise Sales Analysis
- KPI-Based Performance Monitoring

## Business Insights

1. **Overall Performance:** The business generated total sales of **2.30M**, total profit of **286.40K**, approximately **5K orders**, and **38K units sold**.

2. **Monthly Sales Trends:** Sales peaked in **November (0.35M)**, followed by December (0.33M) and September (0.31M). February recorded the lowest sales at approximately 0.06M, indicating seasonal fluctuations in sales.

3. **Regional Performance:** The **West region** recorded the highest sales at approximately **725.46K**, followed by East (678.78K), Central (501.24K), and South (391.72K). The West region also generated the highest profit at approximately **108K**.

4. **Top-Selling Products:** The **Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier** was the leading product by sales at approximately **62K**, followed by the Fellowes PB500 Electric Punch Plastic Comb Binding Machine at 27K.

5. **Category Performance:** **Technology** led category sales at approximately **0.84M**, followed by Furniture (0.74M) and Office Supplies (0.72M).

6. **Customer Segments:** The **Consumer segment** contributed the largest share of sales at approximately **1.16M**, followed by Corporate (0.71M) and Home Office (0.43M).

7. **Sales and Profitability:** The Sales vs Profit chart shows differences in profitability across products. Some products generate high sales but relatively low or negative profit, highlighting the importance of monitoring profit alongside revenue.


## Business Recommendations
- Monitor monthly sales trends to identify performance changes.
- Compare regional profitability to identify improvement opportunities.
- Use product performance to support sales and inventory planning.
